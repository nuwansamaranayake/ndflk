# Department of Social Services: certificate of registration of the National Development Foundation as an NGO, 1994

Method: transcribed by Claude from a scanned image on 2026-10-06. Typed entries on a printed form. Verify against the original.

Reference: SS/NGO/2/94

CERTIFICATE OF REGISTRATION OF NON-GOVERNMENTAL ORGANIZATIONS UNDER THE MONITORING OF RECEIPTS AND DISBURSEMENTS OF FUNDS REGULATION No. 1 OF 1993 PROMULGATED UNDER SECTION 5 OF THE PUBLIC SECURITY ORDINANCE CHAPTER 40

In accordance with the above regulations made by His Excellency the President under Section 5 of the Public Security Ordinance Chapter 40, I, Nawimana Widanage Edmund Wijewanthe, Director of Social Services, certify that NATIONAL DEVELOPMENT FOUNDATION of [street address withheld, private home], Maharagama is registered as a Non-Governmental Organization under the Monitoring of Receipts and Disbursements of Funds by Non-Governmental Organizations Regulation No. 1 of 1993 with effect from 25.03.1994.

Registered No.: 0025
Signed: Director of Social Services, Dept. of Social Services, No. 98, D. S. Senanayake Mawatha, Colombo 8.
Date: 29.03.1994
Printed by: Department of Government Printing
