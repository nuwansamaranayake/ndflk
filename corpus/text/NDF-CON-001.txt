                 NATIONAL DEVELOPMENT FOUNDATION
                                            THE CONSTITUTION
 Draft for review
 This is a retyped copy of the typewritten English constitution of the National Development Foundation and the
 amendments adopted on 7 September 1987 (pages 24 to 41 of the scanned bundle). The wording follows the original.
 Obvious typing slips are corrected and listed in the Review notes at the end. Grey markers such as [Scan page 25 (typed
 -2-)] show where each original page starts. Words marked [?] were hard to read. Typed page -11- (Article 19) is missing
 from the scan. Please mark any changes and return this copy.

1. NAME
     The Foundation (herein after referred to as the Foundation) shall be known as the National
     Development Foundation.

2. REGISTERED OFFICE
     The registered office of the Foundation is housed at No.5, S.de S. Jayasingha Road, Kohuwala,
     Nugegoda or at a place decided by the Management Council.

3. EMBLEM
     The emblem of the Foundation shall be the emblem depicting a sketch of Sri Lanka on a yellow
     background, with pod of rice and a section of a cog wheel on sides and a "Punkalasa" at the
     centre and words "ජාතික සංවර්‍ධන පදනම" inscribed on the right hand side of the emblem.

4. FLAG
     The flag of the Foundation shall depict a lion with a sword in its right hand, surrounded by a
     rectangular yellow frame with a Bo leaf at each corner, on a red background.

5. AIMS
     The aims of the Foundation shall be as stated below.
      (i)     Conservation, development and educating the people to make equitable use of National
              resources.
      (ii)    Organisation of agrarian life, attainment of the country's self sufficiency through rural
              development and conducting dedicated work to cultivate a spirit of self reliance among
              the peasants.
                                                                                                   [Scan page 25 (typed -2-)]
      (iii)   Instillation of an awareness of their rights in the minds of Sri Lankan citizens, leading the
              people in the spirit of Unity and Fraternity toward the fulfilment of varied functions that
              become necessary in the process of conservation and development of National resources.
      (iv)    Creation of a definite pattern of education with the religious centres at its head, for the
              purpose of promoting spiritual and material development.
      (v)     Establishment of a sense of discipline and a state of self-governing among the people
              through the improvement of their religious, cultural, social and economic standards.
      (vi)    Getting the people acquainted with the rational utilisation of the public funds allocated by
              the State for their benefit.
      (vii)   Adopting necessary measures to protect the national environment.
      (viii) Accomplishment of the tasks that help to move toward an improvement of the masses.

      (ix)    Organisation and execution of all activities that deem necessary for the protection of the
              consumer.
                                                                                            [Scan page 26 (typed -3-)]
      (x)     Adoption of all measures conducive to the preservation of natural creative, economic and
              moral wealth and extension of support for such measures by voluntary and state bodies.
      (xi)    Maintaining relations with national and International organisations in the intent of
              securing the afore mentioned aims.

6. POWERS
     (i)  The Foundation shall have the powers to:- Purchase, obtain on short-term or long-term
          lease or on rent, or receive and retain as donations, all such movable and immovable
          property that by their nature deem necessary for the attainment of all proclaimed aims of
          the Foundation.
      (ii)    Receive and retain all such aid financial or material granted by the Republic of Sri Lanka
              or any other local or foreign institution for the purpose of attaining proclaimed aims of
              the Foundation.
      (iii)   Seek and obtain short-term and long-term credit facilities from the Republic of Sri Lanka
              or any other local or foreign institution and enter into and operate under contracts,
              agreements and bonds etc, with the Republic of Sri Lanka or any other local or foreign
              institution on all occasions when such measures deem necessary for the purpose of
              attaining proclaimed aims of the Foundation.
                                                                                            [Scan page 27 (typed -4-)]
      (iv)    Collect membership dues from the members of the Foundation and levy a service charge
              against aforesaid contracts, agreements and bonds etc, in terms of a percentage
              determined by the Management Council, to supplement its funds in accordance with
              proclaimed aims of the Foundation, and
      (v)     Execute any other function, regarded as necessary by the Management Council for the
              purpose of attaining proclaimed aims of the Foundation.

7. MEMBERSHIP
     Any individual with expressed willingness to channel his dedicated efforts toward realization of
     the aims mentioned in the article (5) of this constitution may forward his or her application, to
     be admitted as a member of the Foundation subjected to the provisions laid down in the article
     (9) of the same constitution.

8. MEMBERSHIP CATEGORIES
     The membership shall consist of the following categories.
      (a)     Honorary membership. (Only the members of the clergy are eligible to become honorary
              members).
      (b)     Ordinary membership.
                                                                                            [Scan page 28 (typed -5-)]
      (c)     Institutional Membership. Institutional membership is conferred only upon those
              institutions or organisations (which are not political parties) which have registered
              themselves under the state or completed three years of successful existence.

9. APPLICATION FOR MEMBERSHIP
     Any individual willing to apply for a membership of the Foundation, under article (8) of the
     Constitution, shall do so by filling in the specified application form obtainable from the Executive
     Secretary of the Management Council of the Foundation and forwarding the same to the
     aforesaid Executive Secretary along with the relevant membership fee. Thereupon the
     Management Council shall consider such application and determine its acceptance or rejection.

10. ACCEPTANCE OR REJECTION OF AN APPLICATION
     The Management Council shall enjoy full authority in accepting or rejecting any application for
     membership without showing causes.

11. MEMBERSHIP FEE
     Ordinary members and institutional members, shall pay an annual membership fee of Rs.25/=
     and Rs.50/= respectively while the honorary members shall remain exempted from paying any
     fee. The membership charges can be revised from time to time in compliance with the wish of the
     Management Council.
                                                                                            [Scan page 29 (typed -6-)]

12. REGISTER OF MEMBERS
      The Executive Secretary of the Management Council shall maintain a Register of members
      consisting of such information as name, membership category, permanent address, date of
      enrolment, date of withholdment and termination of membership and other relevant date [?] of
      each member.

13. VOTE
     (i)     Each member of a category is entitled to cast one vote. However on occasions when any
             member holding ordinary or honorary membership, does at the same time belong to and
             represent any institution that holds institutional membership at the Foundation shall
             have the right to cast only one vote.
      (ii)   All members who are entitled to vote shall enjoy equal rights.

14. REGISTRATION OF MEMBERS
     Any member may resign his membership of the Foundation, after tendering his resignation in
     written form to the Executive Secretary of the Management Council. However the Management
     Council shall have the power to accept or reject such plea for resignation.

15. WITHHOLDMENT OR TERMINATION OF MEMBERSHIP
     The Management Council shall have the power to terminate or withhold for a period determined
     by the Council as justifiable, the membership of any member of the Foundation on one or more of
     the reasons given below.
                                                                                            [Scan page 30 (typed -7-)]
      (a)    Involvement in action that run counter to the proclaimed aims of the Foundation.
      (b)    Abstinence from paying membership dues for three consecutive periods without
             reasonable cause.
      (c)    Involvement in actions, considered by the Foundation as detrimental to its good-name.
      (d)    Violation of the Constitution of the Foundation, and
      (e)    Involvement in actions considered by the Management Council as harmful to the well-
             being of the Foundation.

16. GENERAL CONGRESS
      (i) There shall be a General Congress open to all members of the Foundation.
      (ii)    The General Congress shall meet in sessions at least once in every year. The date of
              commencement of such annual sessions shall be made known to all members of the
              Foundation in the form of a written notice by the Executive Secretary of the Management
              Council at least fourteen days prior to such date, whereas all proposals of the members
              shall be sent to the aforesaid Executive Secretary in written form, to reach him before
              seven days from the date of commencement of such sessions.
      (iii)   The quorum of a session of the General Congress shall be a number not less than one
              fourth of the entire membership of the Foundation.
                                                                                                 [Scan page 31 (typed -8-)]
      (iv)    The Executive Secretary of the Management Council shall convene a special session of the
              General Congress on all occasions when a decision to that effect had been taken by
              aforesaid Council or a written request had been made by the members of the Foundation
              whose number shall not be less than one fourth of the entire membership. Such a written
              request shall show the reason or reasons on which the particular request is made. Further
              the date of commencement of such special sessions shall be made known to all members
              of the Foundation in the form of a written notice by the Executive Secretary of the
              Management Council at least ten days prior to the commencement.
      (v)     The quorum of a special session of the General Congress shall be a number not less than
              one fourth of the entire membership of the Foundation.
      (vi)    On occasions when unanimous decision is not arrived at, the decision of the majority of
              the participants shall become valid at all annual sessions or special sessions of the General
              Congress.
      (vii)   On occasions when the decisions of votes is equal on a particular issue taken up before a
              session of the General Congress, The Chairman presiding at the time shall have the right to
              a deciding vote.
                                                                                                 [Scan page 32 (typed -9-)]
      (viii) The terms - "all members and entire membership" included in this article 16, shall be
             interpreted so as to exclude all members whose memberships are withholden at the time.

17. POWERS OF THE GENERAL CONGRESS
      The powers vested in the responsibility of the General Congress shall be as mentioned below.
      (i)     Election of fifteen members to the Management Council.
      (ii)    Appointment of auditors.
      (iii)   Adoption of the audited financial report and the work-report of the Foundation.

18. MANAGEMENT COUNCIL
     (i)  The General Congress at its annual sessions shall elect the Management Council.
      (ii)    The Management Council elected under section (i) of the article 17 of the constitution
              shall consist of :-
              (a)     Nine individuals on whose merits and special talents considered as qualified by the
                      General Congress.

                (b)     Three members from the full time co-ordinating committee of the Foundation, and
                (c)     Three members from the full time services of the Foundation.
       (iii)    The Management Council at its first meeting shall elect from its members, a Chairman,
                two Vice Chairmen, a Treasurer, and an Executive Secretary.
                                                                                                      [Scan page 33 (typed -10-)]
       The Chairman of the Management Council by virtue of his office shall also hold the offices of the
       Chairman of the Foundation and the General Congress.
       (iv)     The Chairman of the Management Council shall preside over General Congress sessions as
                well as Management Council meetings. However one of the Vice-Chairmen may take the
                chair in his absence.
       (v)      On occasions when the Chairman of the Management Council is temporarily barred from
                executing his duties and functions owing to illness, having gone out of the island or any
                other reason, the Management Council shall elect by the majority of its votes, one of the
                two Vice Chairmen to take charge of the said duties and functions until such time that the
                Chairman could resume back his normal duties and functions.
       (vi)     The membership of any member of the Management Council shall cease to exist if he or
                she had failed to attend three meetings of the said Council consecutively without
                informing in advance or reasonable cause.
       (vii)    The Management Council shall have the power to withhold for a definite period the
                membership or, expel from the membership, any member of the Management Council
                after the adoption of a resolution by the majority of votes at a meeting of the said Council,
                when in the opinion of the said Council, the member in question had violated the
                provisions of the Constitution of the National Development Foundation or Committed an
                act detrimental to the good name of the Foundation.

20. DUTY OF THE CHAIRMAN OF THE MANAGEMENT COUNCIL
     The Chairman of the Management Council shall
       (i)      Carry out properly the decisions of the General Congress and Management Council,
       (ii)     Execute properly all functions contributing to the attainment of proclaimed aims of the

       (iii)    Make urgent decisions, necessary for the well being of the Foundation,
       (iv)     Preside over the General Congress and the Advisory Board.

                Executive Secretary to take his place for the rest of the term.

31. SETTLEMENT OF DISPUTES
      On occasions, when a dispute or a difference of opinion prevails among the membership or
      between a member and the collective with regard to the matters connected with the Foundation,
      the decision of the Management Council shall be final and binding.

      (ii)    All funds of the Foundation shall be deposited in a Bank or Banks approved by the
              Management Council.
      (iii)   Funds of the Foundation shall be used only for the purpose of realization of the
              proclaimed aims of the Foundation and matters contributing to that end.

33. AMENDMENTS TO THE CONSTITUTION
     (i) The Constitution of the Foundation shall not be amended, revised or annulled unless so
         done at an annual session or a special session of the General Congress, convened for the
         said purpose, with a majority of two thirds of the votes of the members present.
      (ii)    The Executive Secretary of the Management Council shall be informed in the form of a
              written notice about such amendment, revision or annulment.

34. ANNUAL REPORT
     The Executive Secretary of the Management Council shall forward to the Registrar of Companies
     an annual report on income, expenditure and movable and immovable property of the
     Foundation, audited by an auditor recognized under the societies ordinance.

D. Points for the NDF team to decide
1.    Registered office (Article 2): No. 5, S. de S. Jayasingha Road, Kohuwala, Nugegoda. The 1987
      meeting was at No. 91, Pagoda Road, Nugegoda. The 1994 certificate gives Maharagama. Which
      address should the constitution now give?
2.    Council size: the 1987 amendment set Article 17 (i) at seven members. Article 18 (ii) still says 9 + 3
      + 3 = 15 members. The Sinhala amendment of 24 October 1988 changes the number to nine. Article
      18 (ii) needs to match the chosen number.
3.    Vice Chairman: since 1987 there is one Vice Chairman, but Article 18 (iv) still says "one of the Vice-
      Chairmen".
4.    Meetings and quorum (Article 25): the English 1987 page says the council meets at least once in
      three months with a quorum of five. The Sinhala 1987 amendment says at least twice in three
      months with a quorum of four, and the Sinhala 1988 amendment sets the quorum back to five.
      Which wording is correct?
5.    Cheque signing (Article 32 iv): the 1987 wording lists the Treasurer, Chairman, Executive Secretary
      or Vice Chairman but does not say how many must sign. A later Sinhala amendment (Amendment
      03, undated) adds an Accountant and a new clause 32 (8) on regional project bank accounts. These
      later changes have no English version.
6.    Annual report (Article 34): the report goes to the "Registrar of Companies". NDF was registered
      under the Societies Ordinance, so this probably should be the Registrar of Societies. Today the
      National Secretariat for NGOs may also need reports.
7.    Membership fees (Article 11): Rs. 25 and Rs. 50 a year are 1980s amounts. The Management
      Council may revise them under the same article.
8.    Honorary membership (Article 8 a) is open only to members of the clergy. Is this still wanted?
9.    Flag (Article 4): a lion with a sword on a red background with Bo leaves at the corners is close to
      the national flag. Please consider whether this is still suitable.
10. Gender: the text uses "his" throughout. A revision could use neutral wording.
11.   A pencilled "(viii)" appears under Article 32 (vii) with no text. Was a further clause intended?
