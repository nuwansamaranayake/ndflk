OUR VISION
An empowered, just and free society where people live in equanimity, enjoy socio-economic, political,
religio-cultural and social rights equally.

MISSION
a.    To work together with the Sri Lankan citizens in close brotherhood for instillation of an
      awareness on sustainable development, conservation and improvement of National resources.
b.    To help facilitate the people to recognize, respect and establish each other's socio-economic,
      religio-cultural and political values.
c.    To build up an awareness among the people to carry out all activities pertaining to spiritual and
      material development.
d.    Maintaining relations with National and International Organisations, people and join with
      National & International Networks for the purpose of reaching the Vision of National
      Development Foundation and to work in the fields of Training, Research and Media.

e.   To work for the sustainable establishment of National Development Foundation with the
     objective of attaining its Vision.

GOALS
1.   Develop Political, Social, Economic, Religious, Cultural and Environmental sustainability.
2.   Awake the people's potentials.
3.   Facilitate people to organise themselves.
4.   Link network and research with persons, families and organisations.
5.   Establish sustainability of National Development Foundation.

OBJECTIVES
1.   Organisation of agrarian life, attainment of the country's self sufficiency through rural
     development and conducting dedicated work to cultivate a spirit of self reliance among peasants.
2.   Facilitating the people to acquaint with the rational utilisation of the public funds allocated by the
     State for their benefit.
3.   Adopting necessary measures to protect the National environment.
4.   Accomplishment of the tasks that help to improve the living standards of the community.
5.   Organisation and execution of all activities that deem necessary for the protection of the
     consumer.
6.   Adoption of all measures conducive to the preservation of natural, creative, economic and moral
     wealth and extension of support for such measures by voluntary and State bodies.

METHODOLOGY
The following methodologies are practiced in the implementation of the development programmes:
     • Self Evaluation
     • Participatory Rural Appraisal
     • Participatory Interactive Development
     • Participatory Monitoring and Development
     • Participatory Strategic Planning

DEVELOPMENT ACTIVITIES
National Development Foundation has implemented various village based development programmes on
Irrigation, Agriculture, Credit Management, Small Enterprises, Animal Husbandry, Women in
Development, Marketing, Nature Conservation, Health & Nutrition, Children's Programmes, Water &
Sanitation etc.
Over the years the National Development Foundation had been instrumental in forming over two
hundred People's Organisations in various parts of the Country to assist it in the successful
implementation of their work programmes. At present the N.D.F. works with the following People's
Organisations in Kurunegala and Puttalam Districts.

RESOURCE PERSONS
The N.D.F. can proudly boast of a rich Pool of Resource Persons, most of whom are professionals. They
provide expertise in the field of Irrigation, Agriculture, Nature Conservation, Marketing Research and
Marketing, Credit Management, Health & Nutrition, Water & Sanitation etc.

CONSULTANCY SERVICE
The N.D.F. has the capacity to offer Consultancy Service to other Non-Government Organisations who
are in need of same. Consultancies are undertaken in the following sphere of activities.
  • Preparation of Project Proposals
  • Project Monitoring & Evaluation
  • Self Evaluation of N.G.Os.
  • Administration
  • Finance Management
  • Marketing Research & Marketing

TRAINING PROGRAMMES
Our Team of Trainers are competent in conducting Training Programmes in Participatory Methodology
using the following methods:
                      Participatory Rural Appraisal                                PRA
                      Participatory Action Research                                PAR
                      Participatory Monitoring & Evaluation                        PME
                      Participatory Technology Development                         PTD
                      Participatory Strategic Planning                             PSP


Field facilities are also available to conduct Case Studies on Participatory Methodology.

2.    Date: "18 years experience" means the booklet dates from about 1997. Should the new version say
      how many years now, or stay as a historical record?
3.    Contact details: the address is a private home, the six-digit phone number is from the old
      numbering, and the e-mail address is probably closed. Please give current details or
      mark them as historical.
4.    People: please confirm the current Executive Secretary, Deputy Chairman and Senior Facilitator,
      or mark these as past office-holders.
5.    Auditors and bankers: are Coopers & Lybrand (now part of PwC) and Dayananda
      Samarawickrema & Co. still the auditors? Is the Bank of Ceylon, Nugegoda account still open?
6.    Registration: the 1994 registration with the Department of Social Services was under a regulation
      that has since been replaced. Please confirm NDF's current registration status before this text is
      used publicly.
7.    Projects 6 and 8 run to 1999 and project 9 is called "ongoing" in 1996-1997. Please add the final
      results and end dates, and any projects after 1997.
8.    Item 2 lists FIT Canada, the Central Bank, Bank of Ceylon and People's Bank as supporters. Please
      confirm the role of each.
9.    Objectives: this booklet lists six objectives. The constitution lists the Foundation's aims in its own
      wording. Which wording should be used going forward?
10. Staff (28) and people's organisations (42, 06, 20, 01): please update to current figures or label them
