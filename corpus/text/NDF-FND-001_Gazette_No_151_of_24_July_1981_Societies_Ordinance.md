# Gazette of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka, 24 July 1981 (Gazette No. 151), page 508

Method: transcribed by Claude from a scanned image on 2026-10-06. Verify against the original.

Header: Part I, Section (I). Government Notifications. THE SOCIETIES ORDINANCE.

The page carries three notifications under the Societies Ordinance (Chapter 123). The third one concerns the National Development Foundation. The Gazette prints the society name in English letters as Sinhala: "Jatika Sangwardana Padanama". This is read as the Sinhala name of the National Development Foundation (ජාතික සංවර්ධන පදනම).

## Text of the notification for "Jatika Sangwardana Padanama"

BY virtue of the powers vested in me by Section 3 and 4 of the Societies Ordinance (Chapter 123) I, Matarage Sirisena Amarasiri, Acting Minister of Trade and Shipping do by this notification:

(1) authorise the purpose for which the Society known as "Jatika Sangwardana Padanama" as a purpose to which the powers and facilities of that Ordinance ought to be extended.

(2) limit the application of that Ordinance to the aforesaid Society by the exemption of the aforesaid Society from the provisions of Sections 5 (1) and 5 (4) of that Ordinance.

M. S. Amarasiri, Acting Minister of Trade and Shipping.
Ministry of Trade and Shipping, "Rakshana Mandiraya", Colombo 2, 07th July, 1981.
